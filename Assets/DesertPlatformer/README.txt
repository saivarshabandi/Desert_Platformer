Desert PLATFORMER (Unity 6, 2D)
1. Copy the inner DesertPlatformer folder into your project's Assets folder.
2. Wait for Unity to compile.
3. Menu: Desert Platformer > Build Level Scene, then press Play.
Controls: A/D or arrows = move, Space/W = jump, R = restart after win/lose.
Features: parallax background, pushable blocks, coins, hazards, 3 hearts, goal flag.
All art is original and generated for this project.
